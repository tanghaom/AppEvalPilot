  • Provide a safe, indoor home.

  • Patience and love for animals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the requirements to adopt?

Adopters must be 18 or older, provide proof of identity and address, and agree to a home check if necessary. We also look for a commitment to providing a loving, permanent home.

What is the adoption fee?

Adoption fees vary by dog (age, medical needs) but typically range from $150 to $400. This fee helps cover spay/neuter, vaccinations, microchip, and other care.

About Pawsitive Connections

Our Mission

To rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome dogs at risk, while promoting responsible pet ownership and compassion for animals in our community.

Our Story

Founded in 2023 by a group of passionate animal lovers, Pawsitive Connections started as a small foster network and has grown into a beacon of hope for countless dogs. We believe every dog deserves a chance at a happy life.
