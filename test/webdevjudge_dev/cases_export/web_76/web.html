The Old Way vs. Green Mart

Problem: Traditional shopping means long queues, impersonal experiences, and inventory headaches for shop owners, leading to food waste.

Solution: Green Mart blends online convenience with offline reliability. Our QR-code system, smart inventory, and eco-friendly focus redefine your grocery experience.

Old Way

  • Long checkout lines
  • Limited personalization
  • Inventory challenges
  • Potential food waste

Green Mart Way

  • Quick QR access
  • Personalized selection
  • Efficient inventory
  • Waste reduction focus

Simple Steps to Smart Shopping

1. Scan QR Code

Enter the store or access our catalog instantly by scanning a QR code with your smartphone.

2. Select & Pay

Browse products, see real-time prices, add to your digital cart, and pay securely online.

3. Tokenized Pickup

Receive a unique token and a time slot for a quick, contactless order pickup at your convenience.
