Our Story

Antonia & Emi Villareal

From Foggia to Raleigh: A Culinary Dream

Antonia Villareal's journey began in Foggia, a charming town in Southern Italy, where she grew up surrounded by the rich aromas and traditions of Italian home cooking. Food was more than sustenance; it was family, celebration, and love. Five years ago, she brought these cherished traditions and a dream to Raleigh, North Carolina, seeking a new life with her family.

In Raleigh, Antonia met Emi, whose family holds a vibrant legacy of Asian culinary arts. Emi's grandparents passed down secret family recipes from generations, full of unique spices and techniques. It was a match made in culinary heaven. Their shared passion for food and family sparked an idea: why not blend their distinct culinary worlds?

Villareal’s Pizzeria: A Fusion of Hearts and Flavors

And so, Villareal’s Pizzeria was born. It's more than just a restaurant; it's a testament to their journey, a celebration of two cultures, and a place where families can come together. Here, you'll find classic Italian pizzas and pastas made with Nonna's love, alongside exciting Asian-inspired dishes and truly unique fusion creations that you won't find anywhere else.

Our philosophy is simple: use the freshest local ingredients, honor traditional cooking methods, and infuse every dish with creativity and passion. We believe in the power of food to connect people, and we invite you to be a part of our extended family. Join us for a meal and experience the magic of Italian warmth and Asian zest, all under one roof.
